About

Academic Profile

Dr. Abdulaziz Attaallah is an Associate Professor of Computer Science at King Abdulaziz University. His academic and leadership profile combines software engineering research, academic governance, digital transformation, enterprise systems, and technology strategy.

  • PhD in Software Engineering — North Dakota State University
  • Master of Computer and Information Science — Gannon University
  • BSc in Computer Science — King Abdulaziz University
