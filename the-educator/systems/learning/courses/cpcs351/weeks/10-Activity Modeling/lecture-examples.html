Lesson 04

Lecture Examples: Beverage & Box Office

Use the lecture’s two compact workflows to see decisions, concurrency, joins, merges, and final nodes working together.

4of 8 pages
Beverage example

Find and prepare a beverage

  • Start with Find Beverage.
  • If coffee is found, Put coffee in filter, Add water to reservoir, and Get cups include work that can proceed concurrently.
  • The preparation threads synchronize before Turn on machine, Brew coffee, and Pour coffee.
  • If there is no coffee, the flow checks for cola; if no cola exists, the process terminates.
  • This example combines guards, parallel work, synchronization, alternative paths, and a final node.
Box Office example

Order processing

  • After Set Up Order, the process branches between [single order] and [subscription].
  • The subscription route forks to Assign Seats and Award Bonus concurrently.
  • The single-order route includes Assign Seats and Charge Credit Card.
  • A join synchronizes the subscription threads; a merge then brings alternative routes together before Mail Package.

Remember

In exam diagrams, first ask whether paths are alternatives or simultaneous work. That tells you whether you need diamonds or synchronization bars.
