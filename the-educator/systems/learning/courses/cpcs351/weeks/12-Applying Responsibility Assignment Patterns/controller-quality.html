Lesson 04

Controller Benefits, Liabilities & Bloat

A controller improves separation only if it coordinates rather than becoming the place where all business logic accumulates.

4of 8 pages

Benefits

Separation of concerns
Presentation and business logic can change more independently.
High cohesion + low coupling
Responsibilities live in more appropriate objects.
Multiple interfaces
Different UIs can reuse the same business coordination.
Change & reuse
Easier enhancement and improved reusability.
Use-case state
Controller can ensure events occur in the correct sequence.

Liabilities

The pattern introduces more classes to design, implement, test and integrate. More importantly, poor responsibility assignment can create a bloated controller.

Symptoms of bloat

  • One controller handles many unrelated actor requests.
  • The controller does everything instead of delegating to business objects.
  • The controller stores many system/domain attributes.

Why the conventional DB-manager design is weak

The chapter warns against moving all work into DBMgr. If DBMgr must know domain details and perform business responsibilities, responsibilities are still misplaced; this reflects a procedural rather than object-oriented mindset.

Controller ≠ do everything

The controller receives and coordinates the system operation. Domain experts should still perform responsibilities that belong to them.
