Requirements Reviews and Agile Principles

Requirements must be checked with technical, domain, and customer perspectives, while agile work keeps detail proportional to current need.

9of 9 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Justify functional and non-functional requirements and apply elicitation/analysis methods to produce software requirements.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Verification asks whether requirements/models are produced correctly; validation asks whether they reflect what customers actually need.
  • Technical reviews include peer review, walkthrough, and inspection; domain experts and customers/users provide additional review perspectives.
  • Review questionnaires and checklists target incompleteness, duplication, inconsistency, ambiguity, and other defects.
  • Agile principles emphasize customer collaboration, high-level early requirements, prioritized progress, and “just barely enough” modeling for the current purpose.
