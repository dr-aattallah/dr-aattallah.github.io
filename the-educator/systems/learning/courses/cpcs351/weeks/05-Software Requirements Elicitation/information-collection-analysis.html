Information Collection and Analysis

Elicitation quality depends on asking the right questions about business, context, processes, problems, users, priorities, and quality concerns.

7of 9 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Justify functional and non-functional requirements and apply elicitation/analysis methods to produce software requirements.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Information sources include customer presentations, literature, business forms, operating procedures, regulations/standards, surveys, interviews, and user stories.
  • Investigate the current business, environment, existing processes, inputs/outputs, problems, goals, users, priorities, and quality/performance/security considerations.
  • Analysis models—formal or informal—help the team understand the application, requirements, and constraints.
