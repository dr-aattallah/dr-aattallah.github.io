Software Project Management

Project management coordinates the work so the system can be delivered within agreed constraints.

10of 11 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Explain the concept of a software lifecycle with an example, including the deliverables produced from each phase.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Management oversees development and SQA activities.
  • Typical activities include effort estimation, planning and scheduling, risk management, and project administration.
  • The purpose is not paperwork; it is coordinated delivery on time and within budget.
