Short-Term vs Long-Term Quality

Immediate customer satisfaction matters, but sustainable structure determines whether quality survives future change.

7of 8 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Define software quality and explain the role of quality-assurance activities in the software process.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Short-term quality asks whether software meets immediate customer needs.
  • Long-term quality asks whether the software can be maintained, understood, adapted, and extended safely.
  • Quick fixes may satisfy today’s requirement while increasing future change cost.
Visual model

See the idea

Now: works for customer
Next release: understandable
Future: adaptable & maintainable
Worked example

Fast patch

Copying validation logic into five screens may fix today’s defect quickly but multiplies future maintenance risk.

Remember

Good engineering balances immediate fitness with long-term evolvability.
