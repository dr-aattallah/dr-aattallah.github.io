Quality Measurements and Metrics

Measurement makes quality discussions more objective, but a metric is useful only when its meaning and context are clear.

4of 8 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Define software quality and explain the role of quality-assurance activities in the software process.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Software measurements are objective quantitative assessments of software attributes; metrics are standardized or interpreted measurements.
  • Examples span requirements, design, implementation, and object-oriented structures.
  • Requirements metrics can assess ambiguity and completeness; implementation metrics can track defects/KLOC; design metrics include fan-in and fan-out.
  • Metrics are indicators, not automatic judgments of quality.
