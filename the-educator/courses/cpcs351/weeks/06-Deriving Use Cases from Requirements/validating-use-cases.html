Validating and Refining Use Cases

A candidate becomes a use case only when it represents a complete, useful business process for an actor.

4of 9 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Use UML use-case notation and style to depict high-level user requirements and write use-case-centric functional specifications.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Validation asks four questions: Is it a business process? Is it initiated by an actor? Does it end with the actor? Does it accomplish something useful for the actor?
  • All four answers should be yes for a complete top-level use case.
  • If a candidate fails, it may be a step, operation, action, rule, or evidence pointing to a larger goal.

Worked example

Withdraw Cash

Insert Card is a step; Verify PIN is an internal/required behavior; Withdraw Cash is the complete customer goal.

Remember

A use case is recognized by completeness and actor value, not merely by having a verb.
