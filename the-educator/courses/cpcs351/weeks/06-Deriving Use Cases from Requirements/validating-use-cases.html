CPCS 351 / Topic 06 / Validate & Refine
The four-test gate

A candidate becomes a use case only after validation.

The lecture supplies four questions. All four should be answered “yes” before treating a candidate phrase as a complete use case.

If the candidate fails, do not simply delete it. It may be a step, an operation, a business rule, or evidence pointing toward a larger actor goal.

Validation gate

Four “yes” answers

1
Is it a business process?

Does it represent a complete domain task rather than one small action?

2
Is it initiated by an actor?

Can an external role trigger or request the task?

3
Does it end with the actor?

Does the actor receive, observe, or acknowledge the outcome?

4
Is the outcome useful?

Does the completed task deliver recognizable value to the actor?

Business process versus internal work

One goal contains many smaller actions

Use caseWithdraw Cash
contains
StepsInsert card · enter PIN · choose amount
contain
Operations & actionsValidate input · check balance · command dispenser

The actor recognizes “Withdraw Cash” as the business task. The internal operations support it but normally do not become separate top-level use cases.

Refinement decisions

What to do when a candidate fails

Merge

Combine small steps into a complete actor goal.

Rename

Replace technical language with a domain outcome.

Reclassify

Record it as a step, rule, data need, constraint, or acceptance condition.

Split

Separate a candidate that hides multiple independent actor goals.

Relationship reminder

Reuse Topic 05—do not decorate the diagram

After validating the goals, use association, «include», «extend», or generalization only when the semantics improve understanding. Review Topic 05 · Use Case Relationships before adding dependencies.

Think like a software engineer

Is “Verify Balance” a use case?

It may be a reusable included use case inside a transaction model, but for a customer it is usually not the complete goal. “Check Balance” and “Withdraw Cash” express recognizable customer outcomes more directly.

Key takeaway

A valid use case is a complete, actor-initiated business task that ends with an observable and useful actor outcome.
