Actors and System Boundaries

The system boundary defines the subject; actors are external roles that interact with that subject.

2of 9 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Use UML use-case notation and style to depict high-level user requirements and write use-case-centric functional specifications.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • An actor denotes a business role, not a named person.
  • Actors are outside the modeled system and interact with it.
  • Actors can be humans, hardware, other systems, or components when they are external to the chosen subject.
  • Identify both actors who initiate tasks and those for whom tasks are performed.

Worked example

ATM

Customer, Maintenance staff, Stocker, and Bank Network can all be actors depending on the chosen ATM system boundary.

Remember

Always ask “external to what?” before deciding whether something is an actor.
