Apply Controller + Expert + Creator Together

Patterns are strongest when they cooperate to distribute responsibilities across a sequence diagram.

7of 8 pages

Scenario — university course registration

A student selects a section and requests registration. The system must verify that the student is eligible, verify that the section has space, create an enrollment, and return a result.

Step 1 · Controller

RegistrationController receives register(studentId, sectionId) from the UI. The UI does not perform the business work itself.

Step 2 · Experts

The controller obtains Student and Section. Student evaluates information it owns about eligibility/prerequisites; Section answers whether a seat is available because it owns capacity/enrollment information.

Step 3 · Creator

Once the conditions pass, assign creation of Enrollment to an object that contains/records enrollments or has the necessary construction information. The exact creator depends on the domain model — this is why Creator is a design rule rather than a fixed class name.

Interaction sketch

UI → RegistrationController → Student / Section → Enrollment creator → Enrollment. The controller coordinates; experts answer questions about their own data; the creator constructs the new domain object.

Pattern combination

Controller: who receives the system event? Expert: who knows enough to perform a responsibility? Creator: who should construct the new object?
