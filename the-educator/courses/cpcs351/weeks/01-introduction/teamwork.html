Teamwork Challenges

Software engineering gives teams shared ways to conceptualize, communicate, and coordinate complex work.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Three recurring teamwork challenges are conceptualization, communication, and coordination.
  • Processes and methodologies provide a shared way to analyze and design.
  • UML and other models provide a common visual language.
  • Tools automate or support methodology steps and collaboration.
