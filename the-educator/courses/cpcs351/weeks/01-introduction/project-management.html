Software Project Management

Software project management oversees the control and administration of development and quality-assurance activities so the software can be delivered on time and within budget.

“Project management is the cockpit: you need visibility before you can steer.”
— Your Software Engineering Mentor

From the Original Lecture

What does project management do?

The lecture states that software project management oversees the control and administration of the development and SQA activities. It includes effort estimation, project planning and scheduling, risk management, project administration, and other management activities.

Core Management Activities

The original management responsibilities

⏱

Effort Estimation

Estimate how much human effort, time, expertise, and other resources are likely to be required.

📅

Planning & Scheduling

Organize tasks, milestones, dependencies, responsibilities, and expected delivery dates.

⚠

Risk Management

Identify uncertain events that may harm the project, evaluate their impact, and plan responses.

🗂

Project Administration

Coordinate people, decisions, communication, reporting, documentation, changes, meetings, and project records.

The Balancing Act

Every project operates under constraints

TIME
When must it be ready?
COST
What resources can we spend?
SCOPE
What must we deliver?
PROJECT
CONTROL

These constraints influence one another. Adding major scope late in the project may require more time, more resources, reduced scope elsewhere, or acceptance of additional risk.

Modern Example

Managing a university registration system

One project · many management decisions

The release must be ready before registration opens

Effort Estimation Estimate the effort required for prerequisite logic, integrations, authentication, UI work, performance testing, migration, and support.
Scheduling Sequence requirements, design, development, integration, testing, security review, acceptance, and production preparation around the academic calendar.
Risk Management Track risks such as delayed SIS integration, performance bottlenecks, security defects, incomplete requirements, or unavailable infrastructure.
Administration Coordinate developers, testers, university stakeholders, infrastructure teams, meetings, approvals, change requests, and status reporting.
Mentor Insight

Project management does not replace technical engineering. It makes technical work visible, coordinated, prioritized, and controllable so development and SQA can reach the project objective.

Key Takeaway

Software project management oversees development and SQA through estimation, planning and scheduling, risk management, administration, and related control activities to help deliver the software on time and within budget.

Think Like a Software Engineer
