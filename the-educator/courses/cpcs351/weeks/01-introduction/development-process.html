Software Development Process

The development process transforms an initial system concept into an operational system in its target environment.

7of 11 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Explain the concept of a software lifecycle with an example, including the deliverables produced from each phase.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • It identifies business needs and assesses feasibility.
  • It formulates requirements or capabilities the system must deliver.
  • It designs, implements, tests, and deploys the system.
  • Artifacts from one activity become inputs to later activities.

Worked example

Remember

A development process turns an idea into a deployed system through connected engineering activities.
