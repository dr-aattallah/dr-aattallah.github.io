Why Software Engineering?

Scale, complexity, dependence, and teamwork make ad-hoc development insufficient for real systems.

4of 11 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Explain the concept of a software lifecycle with an example, including the deliverables produced from each phase.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Software is used across nearly every sector and systems continue to grow in size and complexity.
  • Large systems require many engineers to work together; individual programming does not scale to organizational development.
  • Engineering is needed because failures can affect business, safety, security, reputation, and cost.
