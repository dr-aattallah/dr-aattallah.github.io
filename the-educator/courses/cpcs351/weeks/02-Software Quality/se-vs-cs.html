Software Engineering vs Computer Science

The fields overlap, but they emphasize different questions: computing foundations and optimal solutions versus reliable development under real constraints.

8of 8 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Define software quality and explain the role of quality-assurance activities in the software process.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Computer science studies computation, algorithms, formal foundations, and efficient solutions.
  • Software engineering focuses on building and evolving software systems with teams, processes, quality, cost, schedule, and stakeholder constraints.
  • Real projects need both: sound computing ideas and disciplined engineering.
Visual model

See the idea

Computer Science → computational principles
Software Engineering → reliable delivery at scale
Worked example

Route planner

CS helps choose an efficient shortest-path algorithm; SE ensures the planner’s requirements, interfaces, testing, deployment, maintainability, and team coordination.

Remember

Software engineering operationalizes computing knowledge within the realities of product development.
