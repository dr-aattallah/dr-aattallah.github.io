Requirements Elicitation Process

Elicitation is a loop of discovery, modeling, formulation, feasibility, review, and prioritization.

3of 9 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Justify functional and non-functional requirements and apply elicitation/analysis methods to produce software requirements.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Identify current problems, business goals, users, environment, and needs.
  • Collect information through presentations, current-operation study, surveys, interviews, literature, forms, procedures, regulations, and standards.
  • Construct analysis models to improve understanding.
  • Derive requirements and constraints, then prioritize them with stakeholders.
  • Assess feasibility and review for correctness, completeness, consistency, and agreement.

Worked example

Clinic scheduling

Interview receptionists, observe current booking, model the workflow, derive slot/notification requirements, assess integration feasibility, then review with users.

Remember

Elicitation is iterative learning: collect evidence, model understanding, formulate requirements, then check that understanding.
