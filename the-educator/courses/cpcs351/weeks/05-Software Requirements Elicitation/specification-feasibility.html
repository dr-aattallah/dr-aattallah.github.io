Requirements Specification and Feasibility

A requirements specification organizes agreed capabilities and constraints; feasibility asks whether they are practical under real limits.

8of 9 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Justify functional and non-functional requirements and apply elicitation/analysis methods to produce software requirements.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • SRS structures commonly include purpose/scope, product context/functions/users, constraints/assumptions, interfaces, functional requirements, performance, design constraints, quality attributes, and appendices.
  • Feasibility considers functional/performance/quality constraints, technology adequacy, timing, cost, and constraints from customers, industry, or government.
  • A requirement can be desirable yet infeasible within available technology, budget, schedule, or policy.

Worked example

Face-recognition check-in

The capability may be technically possible but rejected because privacy regulation, accuracy, hardware cost, or schedule makes it infeasible.

Remember

Specification records what is required; feasibility determines what can responsibly be committed to.
