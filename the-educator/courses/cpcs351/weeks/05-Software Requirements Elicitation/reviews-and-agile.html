Check the specification and the need

Review before assumptions become defects.

Requirements reviews test both the quality of the specification and its alignment with stakeholder intent. Different reviewers reveal different kinds of weakness.

Verification asks whether the requirements work product is built properly. Validation asks whether it describes the right product.

Verification and validation

Two questions protect the project

Verification

Are we building the product right? Examine the quality and correctness of the requirements process, models, and specification.

Validation

Are we building the right product? Confirm that requirements represent what customers and users actually need.

Three review perspectives

Each reviewer sees a different risk

Technical review

Peers use questionnaires, walkthroughs, or inspections to find ambiguity, inconsistency, duplication, and technical weakness.

Expert review

Domain experts check terminology, policies, business rules, safety, regulation, and domain correctness.

Customer/user review

Customers and users confirm goals, priorities, usability expectations, and fitness for real work.

Think like a software engineer

Can an Agile team skip requirements reviews?

No. Reviews may become smaller and more frequent—backlog refinement, example discussions, prototype feedback, and acceptance-criteria review—but verification and validation remain necessary.
