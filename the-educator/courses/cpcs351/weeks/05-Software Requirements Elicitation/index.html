Software Requirements Elicitation

Requirements elicitation discovers and formulates the real capabilities and constraints the future system must satisfy.

1of 9 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Justify functional and non-functional requirements and apply elicitation/analysis methods to produce software requirements.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Requirements are capabilities the system must deliver and are documented in a requirements specification.
  • The planning phase includes requirements elicitation, deriving use cases, and producing an iterative development plan.
  • Elicitation requires close collaboration with customers and users to understand needs, identify real requirements, and prioritize them.
  • Typical activities include problem/need identification, analysis modeling, requirements formulation, feasibility study, checking correctness/consistency, acceptance-test thinking, and planning.

Worked example

University advising

“Students complain about long queues” is a problem, not yet a requirement. Elicitation investigates causes and derives capabilities such as appointment booking and queue visibility.

Remember

Elicitation converts messy stakeholder reality into agreed, testable system capabilities.
