Choosing the Right Model

Model selection is a contextual engineering decision, not a universal ranking.

9of 11 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Describe the different software development models: waterfall, prototype, evolutionary, spiral, and agile.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Relevant factors include unclear requirements, unfamiliar technology, complexity, reliability, schedule, management capability, cost constraints, visibility, team skill, documentation needs, and reuse.
  • Treat selection tables as classroom heuristics—not scientific laws.
  • Projects often combine practices rather than follow one pure textbook model.

Worked example

Medical-device software vs event app

The medical device may require strong plan-driven evidence and risk controls; the event app may benefit from rapid prototypes and short iterations.

Remember

Choose the process logic that best manages the project’s dominant uncertainty and constraints.
