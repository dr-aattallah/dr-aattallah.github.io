Spiral Model

Spiral development is iterative, but its defining logic is risk-driven commitment.

7of 11 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Describe the different software development models: waterfall, prototype, evolutionary, spiral, and agile.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Each cycle establishes objectives and alternatives.
  • Risks are analyzed and important uncertainties are reduced before larger commitments.
  • The next product level is developed and verified.
  • The team evaluates results and plans the next cycle.
  • The model is influential because it makes risk management an explicit driver of iteration.

Remember

Spiral asks: what is the biggest uncertainty, and what should we learn before committing more?
