SELF-CHECK · UNGRADED

Practice before the assessed work

  1. Define two concepts from this unit without using a memorized phrase.
  2. Apply one concept to RoomNow and explain a trade-off.
  3. Find a weakness in the provided example and propose a correction.
  4. State what evidence would convince you that the correction worked.
  5. Apply the same reasoning to a different university system.

These questions are self-study practice only; the graded Quick Check is delivered separately in Blackboard.

GUIDED APPLICATION · FORMATIVE

Guided requirements clinic — M0 readiness

In pairs, critique three ambiguous RoomNow statements. For each, identify the stakeholder, rewrite as a testable FR or NFR, and name a validation question. Then check whether your team's M0 scope and initial backlog are realistic.

EXPECTED EVIDENCEFormative: clear source, measurable outcome where appropriate, consistency with scope and evidence of stakeholder validation.

Instructor preparation: W3 M1: remind students of M0 scope/repository/team agreement; W3 M3: allow a short clinic before QC2 and M0 submission.

Important: This is formative guided practice, not an additional graded assignment. Official Quick Checks and milestones are recorded in Blackboard.
