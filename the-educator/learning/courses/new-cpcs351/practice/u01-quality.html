The team says RoomNow is easy to change. Name an observation that would support or challenge this maintainability claim.

Ungraded self-study practice. Your text is lost on reload; copy it before leaving, or answer on paper.

Compare your reasoning, then revise

Choose a representative change and observe its effort, affected dependencies and regression results. A claim such as “we use a framework” names a technique, not evidence that change is manageable. No universal numeric target is established by this scenario.

Revise: add the stakeholder, uncertainty or observable evidence missing from your first response. Different justified answers are possible.
