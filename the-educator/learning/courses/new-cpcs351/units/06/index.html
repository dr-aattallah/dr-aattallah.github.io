CONCEPT 1

Design as decisions

Design translates requirements and constraints into responsibilities, interfaces and components. Good decisions can be explained against alternatives.

ROOMNOWShould booking validation live in a UI screen or a service used by every booking channel?
Practice the engineering judgment

What failure could occur if this principle is ignored? Propose a better decision and describe how you would verify it.

CONCEPT 2

Separation of concerns

Keep responsibilities with coherent purposes to reduce accidental coupling and change impact.

ROOMNOWUI displays booking choices; booking service enforces booking rules; persistence stores data.
Practice the engineering judgment

What failure could occur if this principle is ignored? Propose a better decision and describe how you would verify it.

CONCEPT 3

Coupling and cohesion

Aim for focused modules with clear dependencies; neither metric is a magic score.

ROOMNOWA single GodClass handling login, booking, payment and notifications has weak cohesion.
Practice the engineering judgment

What failure could occur if this principle is ignored? Propose a better decision and describe how you would verify it.

CONCEPT 4

Interfaces and information hiding

Define what a component promises without exposing unnecessary internals; changes should remain local where practical.

ROOMNOWBookingService exposes reserve(request) without exposing the database schema to callers.

CONCEPT 5

Architecture views and rationale

Use a simple component view, identify external systems and document why the structure addresses key quality attributes.

ROOMNOWSSO is an external dependency; design for failure and do not store credentials in RoomNow.

CONCEPT 6

One pattern in context

Introduce one illustrative design pattern only when it solves a real variation; avoid pattern catalogs.

ROOMNOWA strategy for alternative booking eligibility policies can isolate rule changes if variations are expected.

APPLICATION · CLASS / PROJECT

Apply and transfer

Studio 3: review a weak RoomNow design; identify coupling, privacy and testability issues; propose a clearer responsibility allocation with rationale.

Evidence standard: an understandable decision, a specific artifact or link where appropriate, a verification method, and a brief justification. Studios are formative; Challenges are individual graded work when scheduled.

Self-check (ungraded)

  1. State two concepts and distinguish their purposes.
  2. Explain one RoomNow design or workflow trade-off.
  3. Identify a defect or unsupported claim in a sample artifact.
  4. Suggest a correction and evidence that could validate it.
  5. Transfer the idea to a different system.
