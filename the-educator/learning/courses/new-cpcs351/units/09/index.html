CONCEPT 1

Security and privacy as requirements

Identify assets, trust boundaries, misuse scenarios, least privilege and privacy exposure early.

ROOMNOWA student must not cancel another student's booking; logs must not reveal unnecessary personal information.
Practice the engineering judgment

What failure could occur if this principle is ignored? Propose a better decision and describe how you would verify it.

CONCEPT 2

Threat thinking

Describe actor, asset, abuse action, impact and a proportionate control; avoid pretending one checklist solves all risks.

ROOMNOWThreat: unauthorized cancellation; control: enforce booking ownership server-side; verify with negative tests.
Practice the engineering judgment

What failure could occur if this principle is ignored? Propose a better decision and describe how you would verify it.

CONCEPT 3

Professional judgment

Balance obligations to users, stakeholders, safety, privacy and truthful reporting. Escalate unresolved serious risks.

ROOMNOWDo not claim an audit log exists if the implementation has no evidence.
Practice the engineering judgment

What failure could occur if this principle is ignored? Propose a better decision and describe how you would verify it.

CONCEPT 4

AI-assisted engineering

AI may propose requirements, code or tests, but the human engineer checks correctness, privacy, bias and provenance.

ROOMNOWA generated test may assert the wrong business rule; compare it to stakeholder-approved acceptance criteria.
Practice the engineering judgment

What failure could occur if this principle is ignored? Propose a better decision and describe how you would verify it.

CONCEPT 5

Verify and justify

Use Generate → Critique → Verify → Justify; document what was accepted, corrected or rejected.

ROOMNOWReject an AI suggestion to upload real booking data to a public service; use synthetic examples.

APPLICATION · CLASS / PROJECT

Apply and transfer

Studio 6: evaluate AI-generated requirements/test/code artifacts and verify claims with evidence. Challenge 3 occurs separately in W14 M1.

Evidence standard: an understandable decision, a specific artifact or link where appropriate, a verification method, and a brief justification. Studios are formative; Challenges are individual graded work when scheduled.

Self-check (ungraded)

  1. State two concepts and distinguish their purposes.
  2. Explain one RoomNow design or workflow trade-off.
  3. Identify a defect or unsupported claim in a sample artifact.
  4. Suggest a correction and evidence that could validate it.
  5. Transfer the idea to a different system.
