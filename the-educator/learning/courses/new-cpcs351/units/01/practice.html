Five decisions to test your understanding

This is ungraded practice.

? Questions01 / 05 · QUALITY & PROFESSIONAL PRODUCT

RoomNow passes its demo, but changing one booking policy requires risky edits across 17 files. Which investigation best addresses the missing evidence of product quality?

Reasoning: Maintainability is the primary concern. Professional product quality includes safe evolution, not just successful execution today. Seventeen files are a warning to investigate, not proof of a particular architectural defect.
? Questions02 / 05 · SYSTEM BOUNDARY

University IT operates SSO. RoomNow cannot sign users in during an SSO outage. Which allocation of responsibility is most defensible?

Reasoning: A boundary separates scope and ownership, not relevance. RoomNow must avoid unauthorized bookings and misleading success while its external dependency is unavailable.
? Questions03 / 05 · PROCESS & DISCIPLINES

Users reject a pilot booking rule. Which response best applies software engineering rather than treating this only as an algorithm problem?

Reasoning: Computer science foundations support the solution; software engineering also coordinates requirements, implementation and validation under real constraints. These activities need not form a single linear pass.
? Questions04 / 05 · CONTEXT & ENGINEERING JUDGMENT

Policy requires MFA for room administrators, while users report login difficulty. Which decision respects both the constraint and the need for evidence?

Reasoning: Context does not make every option equally acceptable. Respect mandatory controls, identify viable alternatives and compare threat impact, usability and accessibility evidence before choosing.
? Questions05 / 05 · PROFESSIONAL RESPONSIBILITY

A public AI service could help debug RoomNow, but institutional approval for uploading booking records has not been established. What should the engineer do?

Reasoning: Confidentiality remains an engineering responsibility. Sanitization alone is not permission. If records were already sent, follow the institution’s incident reporting and containment process rather than continuing the upload.
