UNIT 01 · REMEMBER

Eight ideas to carry into the rest of CPCS351.

01Professional software is more than executable code.
02Software engineering covers the whole software life, not only implementation.
03Engineering methods are selected for context; there is no universal best method.
04Quality includes maintainability, dependability/security, efficiency and acceptability.
05Specify, develop, validate and evolve are fundamental activities.
06Software lives inside larger technical and social systems.
07Heterogeneity, change, trust/security and scale continuously pressure engineering.
08Professional responsibility is part of engineering judgment.
ACADEMIC SOURCES & FURTHER STUDY

Unit 01 evidence base

  1. Main textbook: Ian Sommerville, Software Engineering, 10th ed., Chapter 1 — Introduction. Primary basis for professional software development, essential software attributes, software engineering definition, fundamental activities, diversity, challenges, systems relationship and ethics.
  2. Professional body of knowledge: IEEE Computer Society, Guide to the Software Engineering Body of Knowledge (SWEBOK Guide), v4.0a — especially Software Quality, Software Engineering Professional Practice, Software Engineering Process, Software Security and Engineering Foundations.
  3. Modern companion: Ian Sommerville, Engineering Software Products — useful bridge to product-oriented, agile, security, testing and DevOps practice used later in this course.

RoomNow, CampusCare and the evidence fixtures are locally authored teaching adaptations; their hypothetical decisions are not recommendations from the references. Author’s chapter presentations (new tab) · IEEE SWEBOK resource (new tab).

Reading target for this unit: Sommerville 10e, Chapter 1, with emphasis on §1.1 Professional Software Development and §1.2 Software Engineering Ethics. Case studies in §1.3 provide transfer examples showing why engineering practice depends on system type.
