CONCEPT 1

What tests establish

Testing gathers evidence about behavior and quality. It can reveal faults but cannot prove total absence of defects.

ROOMNOWPassing the normal booking test does not establish correct handling of overlapping reservations.
Practice the engineering judgment

From criteria to tests

Convert each acceptance criterion into representative normal, boundary and failure tests.

Automated unit tests

Automate deterministic checks of small units; isolate dependencies where appropriate and inspect failures rather than merely counting green tests.

CONCEPT 4

Levels and risk

Different levels address different failure modes: unit, integration, system and acceptance. Choose evidence based on the claim.

ROOMNOWAn SSO integration failure is not detected by a unit test of time-slot overlap.

CONCEPT 5

Regression and evidence

Retest affected behavior after change and keep concise links to results, limitations and unresolved risk.

APPLICATION · CLASS / PROJECT

Apply and transfer

Studio 5: derive and implement tests for one real acceptance criterion; interpret a failing test and document the evidence.

Evidence standard: an understandable decision, a specific artifact or link where appropriate, a verification method, and a brief justification. Studios are formative; Challenges are individual graded work when scheduled.

Self-check (ungraded)

  1. State two concepts and distinguish their purposes.
  2. Explain one RoomNow design or workflow trade-off.
  3. Identify a defect or unsupported claim in a sample artifact.
  4. Suggest a correction and evidence that could validate it.
  5. Transfer the idea to a different system.
