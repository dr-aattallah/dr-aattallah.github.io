Prepare your reasoning

  1. Identify the problem or unsupported claim.
  2. Analyze its engineering consequences.
  3. Propose an improvement.
  4. Explain how you would verify it.
  5. Justify the decision and acknowledge limitations.

These are the existing challenge protocol, not a new point-by-point marking scheme.
