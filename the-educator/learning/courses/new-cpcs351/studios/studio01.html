Studio 1 — Team Workflow

In teams, create or join one repository, a simple board, a working agreement and three issues for RoomNow or your approved project. Each issue must state an observable completion condition. Assign owners, prioritize work, and explain why the first item should be done first.

EXPECTED EVIDENCEFormative: working links, understandable issues, realistic ownership, clear acceptance criteria, one justified priority decision.

Instructor preparation: Before W2 M3: GitHub accounts ready; instructor prepares a sample repository and fallback screenshots. W2 M2 last 10 minutes: explain goal, deliverables, roles and access check.

Important: This is formative guided practice, not an additional graded assignment. Official Quick Checks and milestones are recorded in Blackboard.

Work, review, transfer

  1. Read the brief and agree who will explain, produce and review the work.
  2. Produce the evidence required by the brief. Keep decisions and working links understandable.
  3. Review whether the evidence supports the decision; identify one remaining limitation.
  4. Transfer useful work to your existing project. Do not create a duplicate assignment.

This is a local workflow aid, not an additional grading rubric.
