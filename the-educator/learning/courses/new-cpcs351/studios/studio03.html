APPLICATION · CLASS / PROJECT

Apply and transfer

Studio 3: review a weak RoomNow design; identify coupling, privacy and testability issues; propose a clearer responsibility allocation with rationale.

Evidence standard: an understandable decision, a specific artifact or link where appropriate, a verification method, and a brief justification. Studios are formative; Challenges are individual graded work when scheduled.

Work, review, transfer

  1. Read the brief and agree who will explain, produce and review the work.
  2. Produce the evidence required by the brief. Keep decisions and working links understandable.
  3. Review whether the evidence supports the decision; identify one remaining limitation.
  4. Transfer useful work to your existing project. Do not create a duplicate assignment.

This is a local workflow aid, not an additional grading rubric.
