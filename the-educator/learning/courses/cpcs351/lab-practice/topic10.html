Mission 10 — Make Five Models Tell One Story

Jeddah Mobility is launching ParkFlow, a smart parking reservation platform for public parking facilities. A driver searches for a facility, reserves an available space, receives a time-limited entry code, enters the facility, occupies the assigned space, pays, exits, and receives confirmation. If the code is invalid or expired, entry must be blocked. You are the lead model reviewer. Choose the right UML view, trace requirements across models, detect contradictions, and approve only a coherent set of diagrams.
