WHAT THE INFOGRAPHIC MEANS IN PRACTICE

Three things to get right

1 · Evidence, not just information

Use credible academic and technical sources, compare what they say and connect evidence to your research question.

2 · Analysis, not just summary

Explain relationships, strengths, weaknesses, gaps, implications and your supported insights rather than listing facts.

3 · One coherent argument

Your introduction, literature review, analysis, conclusion and references should work together to answer one focused question.

Minimum requirement: at least five content pages, excluding the cover page and references. The work is individual and must use IEEE-style citations and references.

BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

Final quality gate

Required

  • Individual Word document.
  • Five or more content pages, excluding cover and references.
  • IEEE citations with matching references.
  • Submission through the designated course portal.

Check your argument

  • Is the research question clear and actually answered?
  • Are important claims supported by credible evidence?
  • Did you analyze rather than only summarize?
  • Are technical terms, citations and references accurate?
