01

What this assignment develops

Research, critical analysis, technical accuracy and written communication.

Investigate

Understand a software engineering question deeply enough to explain its concepts, context, trade-offs and practical significance.

Analyze

Compare evidence, identify relationships and limitations, and build conclusions that follow logically from what you found.

Communicate

Present a coherent technical argument using accurate terminology, logical organization, credible sources and IEEE citations.

02

Research workflow

A practical path from an assigned topic to a defensible paper.

STEP 1Decode the question

Identify concept, scope and relationship.

STEP 2Build evidence

Find credible academic and technical sources.

STEP 3Analyze

Compare findings, benefits and limitations.

STEP 4Write

Build a logical evidence-based argument.

STEP 5Validate

Check accuracy, citations, originality and clarity.

03

Recommended paper architecture

A clear academic flow for the required research paper.

Cover Page

Course, research title, student information and submission details.

1 · Introduction

Problem, scope, significance and research question.

Essential software engineering concepts and terminology.

Present, compare, interpret and connect credible evidence.

4 · Discussion

Implications, trade-offs, challenges and practical consequences.

5 · Conclusion & Recommendations

Answer the question and synthesize supported recommendations.

References

Every cited source listed in IEEE reference style.

Minimum Length

At least five content pages, excluding cover and references.

05

Evidence, citation & academic integrity

Credibility, traceability and professional presentation are part of the engineering argument.

Choose credible evidence

Prefer scholarly publications, standards, authoritative technical documentation and reputable professional sources.

Use IEEE documentation

Cite borrowed ideas using IEEE-style numbered citations and provide matching full references.

Write originally

Read, understand, synthesize and explain in your own academic voice. Proper acknowledgment is mandatory.

Official IEEE Word Resource

IEEE Sample Word Template

A practical Microsoft Word example from IEEE Power & Energy Society. Use it as a visual and structural reference when preparing your paper so you can see how an IEEE-style manuscript is organized and formatted in Word.

  • Use it to understand page layout, headings and manuscript structure.
  • Compare your document's organization and visual consistency with an IEEE example.
  • Treat it as a formatting aid—not as content to copy.

Official IEEE Writing Guide

IEEE Editorial Style Manual for Authors

The official IEEE author style manual explains how IEEE technical articles should be written and presented. It covers article organization, abstracts, headings, citations and references, figures and tables, equations, grammar, terminology, abbreviations and other editorial conventions.

  • Use it when you are unsure how to structure or phrase part of the paper.
  • Check IEEE conventions instead of guessing formatting and writing rules.
  • Use the relevant sections selectively; this course paper does not require every publication-specific element used by IEEE journals.

07

Submission & final quality check

What must be true before you submit.

Submission requirements

  • Complete the work individually.
  • Submit a Word document through the designated course portal.
  • Meet the five-page minimum excluding cover and references.
  • Follow the required structure and IEEE citations.
  • Late work follows the course late-submission policy.

Researcher's final check

  • Is the research question clear?
  • Are major claims supported by credible evidence?
  • Did you analyze rather than only summarize?
  • Are technical terms accurate?
  • Do citations match the reference list?
  • Does the conclusion answer the question?
