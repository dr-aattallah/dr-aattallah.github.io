Swimlanes & Responsibility

Partition a workflow by the class, actor, department, or subsystem responsible for each activity.

6of 8 pages
Core lecture

Responsibility partitions

  • Activity diagrams are often associated with several classes or organizational roles.
  • Swimlanes divide the diagram into responsibility zones.
  • Each zone represents a particular class, department, actor, or subsystem.
  • The lecture’s course-registration diagram partitions Student and CourseSection responsibilities.
  • The combined order-processing diagram partitions Finance, Order Processing, and Stock Manager.
  • The later customer-order diagram uses Customer, Sales, Accounting, and Warehouse and also shows object flow.
