Agile Methods

Agile is a family of methods and practices, not one single recipe. Different methods emphasize different ways of planning, engineering, communicating and improving flow.

10of 11 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Recognize major Agile methods and explain the distinctive emphasis each brings to an Agile project.

Six methods · six hooks

Same Agile family, different emphasis

⏱️
DSDM

Emphasizes disciplined delivery within fixed time and resources, with active user involvement and prioritization.

🧩
Feature Driven Development

Organizes progress around small, client-valued features and short design/build cycles.

🏃
Scrum

Provides a concrete iterative management framework with short cycles, prioritized work and regular inspection/adaptation.

🛠️
Extreme Programming (XP)

Strengthens technical discipline through frequent integration, testing, refactoring and close feedback.

💎
Crystal Clear

Emphasizes people, communication and tailoring the process to team size and project criticality.

✂️
Lean Development

Focuses on reducing waste, improving flow, delivering value and avoiding unnecessary work or delay.

Real teams combine practices

Three memory anchors

What should you remember?

📅 Scrum = cadence

Think iterations, prioritized work, reviews and adaptation.

🧪 XP = engineering discipline

Think frequent testing, integration and refactoring.

✂️ Lean = flow

Think value, waste reduction and shorter delays.

Worked example

Product team

A team might use Scrum for planning and coordination, XP practices for testing/refactoring, and Lean ideas to reduce waiting and handoffs. That combination is not a contradiction—it is a practical use of complementary Agile ideas.

Remember

Agile principles can be implemented through different methods and combined practices. The method name matters less than understanding what problem each practice is trying to solve.

Exam lens

Recognize the dominant emphasis

If the scenario emphasizes...

Iterations and team workflow → think Scrum. Strong technical practices → think XP. Waste and flow → think Lean.

Do not confuse

“Agile” is the broad family. Scrum, XP, DSDM, FDD, Crystal and Lean are methods or approaches within that broader space.
