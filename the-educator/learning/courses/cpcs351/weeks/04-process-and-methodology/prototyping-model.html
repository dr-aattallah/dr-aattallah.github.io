Prototyping Model

A prototype is built early to learn what users actually need before committing to the complete solution.

5of 11 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Describe the different software development models: waterfall, prototype, evolutionary, spiral, and agile.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Instead of freezing uncertain requirements, a working model is built quickly and shown to users.
  • Feedback exposes missing, misunderstood, or unusable requirements early.
  • Throwaway/rapid prototypes are discarded after learning; evolutionary prototypes can become the basis of the production system.
  • The prototype is a learning instrument, not automatically production-quality software.

Worked example

Clinic appointment interface

Remember

Prototype to learn; do not confuse “looks real” with “ready for production.”
