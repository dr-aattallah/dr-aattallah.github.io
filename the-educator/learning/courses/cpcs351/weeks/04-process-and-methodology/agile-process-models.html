Agile Process Models

Agile emphasizes short feedback loops, useful increments, customer collaboration, and adaptation to change.

8of 11 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Describe the different software development models: waterfall, prototype, evolutionary, spiral, and agile.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Work is delivered in short iterations or increments rather than one long release cycle.
  • Changing requirements can be incorporated through repeated planning and feedback.
  • Customer/user collaboration and frequent working software improve visibility.
  • Agile still requires engineering discipline; weak documentation, architecture, testing, or coordination can create long-term problems.
