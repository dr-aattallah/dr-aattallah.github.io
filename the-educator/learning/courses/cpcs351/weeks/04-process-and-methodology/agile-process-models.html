Agile Process Models

Agile emphasizes short feedback loops, useful increments, customer collaboration, and adaptation to change.

8of 11 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Explain how Agile organizes development around short iterations, working increments, feedback, collaboration and adaptation.

Core lecture · Visual anchor

Short cycles turn feedback into the next increment

🧭 Plan
→
🛠 Build

👥 Review

💡 Learn

Worked example

Student services portal

Release appointment booking first, observe real use, review feedback with users, then adjust reminders and waiting-list rules in the next short cycle.

Remember

Agile is not simply “working fast.” It is disciplined adaptation through short feedback and delivery cycles.

Added clarification

Connect it to engineering practice

Why it works

Frequent working increments make progress visible and allow changing requirements to influence the product before too much is committed.

Exam lens

Look for short iterations, working software, customer collaboration, repeated feedback and adaptation. Agile still requires architecture, testing and engineering discipline.
