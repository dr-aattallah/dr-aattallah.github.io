Development Challenges

Different process models exist because projects face different combinations of uncertainty, coordination, change, constraints and technology.

2of 11 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Explain the project and system pressures that make a disciplined software process necessary and connect those pressures to process-model choices.

Core lecture

Why coding alone is not enough

  • Projects may last years while future customer needs remain uncertain.
  • Multiple departments, teams and locations must divide, coordinate and integrate work.
  • Teams may use different processes, methods and tools.
  • Systems must satisfy many requirements and constraints that can change over time.
  • Hardware, software, third-party components, languages, platforms and machines must interoperate.
Pressure → engineering response

Each challenge creates a different need

🌫️UncertaintyNeed learning and feedback before committing too much.
🤝Many TeamsNeed coordination, interfaces, milestones and integration.
🔄ChangeNeed a process that can absorb evolving requirements.
⚠️High RiskNeed explicit analysis and reduction of uncertainty.
🧊StabilityWhen requirements are stable, stronger up-front planning becomes practical.

Why do different process models exist?

Uncertainty → feedbackTeams → coordinationChange → adaptabilityRisk → risk managementStability → planning

Key bridge: process models are alternative engineering responses to different project pressures. The next lesson compares those responses.

Worked example

Smart-city platform

Transit, payments, sensors, mobile apps, vendors and operations teams cannot be coordinated by coding alone. Their uncertainty, interfaces and changing constraints determine what kind of process discipline the project needs.

Remember

Do not ask “Which model is best?” Ask “Which project pressure must the process manage?”
