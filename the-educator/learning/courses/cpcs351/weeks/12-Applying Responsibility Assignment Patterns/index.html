Design patterns: reusable design knowledge

Design patterns are abstractions of proven design solutions to commonly encountered design problems. A pattern solves a class of problems, improves communication among developers, helps less-experienced designers produce stronger designs, and captures reusable design knowledge.

Patterns implement design principles. Pattern-based components tend to be easier to understand, test, maintain and combine when solving larger design problems.

Added study lens

Patterns are decisions, not decorations

Do not add a pattern because its name sounds sophisticated. First state the design problem. Then select a pattern whose responsibility assignment directly improves that problem.

Core question for Topic 12

After discovering objects, which object should do what? GRASP gives general principles for answering that question.
