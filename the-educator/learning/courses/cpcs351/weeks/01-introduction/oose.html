Official Lecture Content

Object-Oriented Software Engineering

OOSE is a specialization of software engineering. The object-oriented paradigm views the world and systems as consisting of objects that relate and interact with each other.

OOSE encompasses:

  • OO processes
  • OO methodologies
  • OO modeling languages
  • OO tools
Visual Anchor · An Interacting Object Universe
Customer↔Order↔Payment↔Inventory

The point is not these particular objects; it is the paradigm: systems are modeled through related objects and their interactions.

Topic 01 · Key Takeaways

Rebuild the Story

1. Software is intangible, cheap to reproduce, labor-intensive, easy to modify, and does not physically wear out.
2. Software engineering applies engineering processes and methods to improve P and Q while reducing C and T.
3. Scale makes teamwork unavoidable; teamwork creates conceptualization, communication and coordination challenges.
4. Software engineering operates through three interacting tracks: Development, SQA and Project Management.
5. Development transforms concept into an operational system; Waterfall is one structured sequence of development phases.
6. OOSE specializes software engineering around objects, relationships and interactions.
