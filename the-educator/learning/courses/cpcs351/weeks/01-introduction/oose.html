Official Lecture Content

Object-Oriented Software Engineering

OOSE is a specialization of software engineering. The object-oriented paradigm views the world and systems as consisting of objects that relate and interact with each other.

OOSE encompasses:

  • OO processes
  • OO methodologies
  • OO modeling languages
  • OO tools
Visual Anchor · An Interacting Object Universe
Customer↔Order↔Payment↔Inventory

The point is not these particular objects; it is the paradigm: systems are modeled through related objects and their interactions.

Topic 01 · Key Takeaways

Rebuild the Story

1. Software is intangible, cheap to reproduce, labor-intensive, easy to modify, and does not physically wear out.
2. Software engineering applies engineering processes and methods to improve P and Q while reducing C and T.
3. Scale makes teamwork unavoidable; teamwork creates conceptualization, communication and coordination challenges.
4. Software engineering operates through three interacting tracks: Development, SQA and Project Management.
5. Development transforms concept into an operational system; Waterfall is one structured sequence of development phases.
6. OOSE specializes software engineering around objects, relationships and interactions.

Reveal a model reasoning path

The product is inexpensive to reproduce but costly to understand, coordinate, test and evolve. Engineering balances performance/quality against cost/time. A team is needed because requirements, UI, data, security, testing and deployment create specialization and coordination needs. Development moves through requirements, design, implementation, integration/testing and operation/evolution; SQA provides quality evidence while PM coordinates scope, people, schedule and resources. An OOSE view might model Student, Course, Section, Registration, Instructor and Payment as related objects with responsibilities.

Exam lens

Distinctions worth being able to explain

Program vs. engineered software

Working code alone is not evidence that requirements, quality, maintainability, coordination and evolution have been engineered.

Life cycle vs. Waterfall

The life cycle is the broader set of activities over software's existence; Waterfall is one way of organizing development phases.

Development vs. SQA vs. PM

They are interacting tracks: build the product, provide quality confidence, and coordinate the work.

SE vs. OOSE

OOSE does not replace software engineering; it specializes it using the object-oriented paradigm.
