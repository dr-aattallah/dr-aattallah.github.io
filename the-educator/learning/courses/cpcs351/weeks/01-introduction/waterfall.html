Waterfall Process

Waterfall provides a clear sequential view of major development stages and the deliverables that connect them.

8of 11 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Explain the concept of a software lifecycle with an example, including the deliverables produced from each phase.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Major stages include system engineering, requirements analysis, design, coding and unit testing, integration testing, acceptance testing, and maintenance.
  • Each stage produces outputs that feed the next stage.
  • The model is useful as a simple life-cycle reference, especially when requirements are stable and coordination/documentation are important.

Worked example

Payroll replacement project

When regulations and requirements are stable, teams can baseline requirements, design against them, implement, verify, and release in planned stages.

Remember

Waterfall emphasizes staged progression and clear deliverables; its main tension is accommodating change after commitment.
