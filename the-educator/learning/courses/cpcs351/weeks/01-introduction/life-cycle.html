Software Life Cycle

A software life cycle coordinates development, quality assurance, and project management across the life of a system.

6of 11 pages
Before You StartDoes quality assurance begin after developers finish, and does project management sit outside technical work?

Use this page to replace that sequential picture with three coordinated tracks.

Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning Focus

Distinguish the overall software life cycle from the development process and explain how development, SQA, and project management operate as coordinated tracks.

Core Lecture

Three Coordinated Life-Cycle Tracks

  • Software processes and methodologies consist of life-cycle activities.
  • Development transforms an initial concept into an operational system.
  • Software Quality Assurance checks both how work is performed and the quality of produced artifacts.
  • Project Management controls effort, schedule, risk, resources, and administration.
Visual Model

Think Parallel, Not Sequential

DEVDevelopmentconcept → operational system
SQASoftware Quality Assuranceprocess + artifact quality throughout
PMProject Managementplan + track + risk + coordination
Worked Example

University Advising System

While developers elicit requirements and build features, SQA reviews requirements and verifies behavior, while project management estimates effort, tracks schedule, manages risk, and coordinates people. The tracks overlap in time.

Remember

The life cycle is a coordinated system of work; development, SQA, and project management support one another throughout it.

Common Misconception

“First we develop, then QA tests, then management closes the project.” That sequence hides the core idea: quality and management activities begin early and continue alongside development.
