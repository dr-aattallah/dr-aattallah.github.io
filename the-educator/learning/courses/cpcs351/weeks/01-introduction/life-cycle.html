Topic 01 · Lesson 05

Software Life Cycle — The Big Picture

Software engineering is not one chain of coding steps. The chapter organizes it as three interacting life-cycle tracks.

5of 8 lessons
Think in parallelWhile developers design and code, who protects quality—and who keeps the project feasible, scheduled and controlled?
Learning Focus

Name the three life-cycle tracks, explain their roles, and show how together they pursue productivity, quality, cost and time-to-market goals.

Visual Anchor · Software Life Cycle Activities

Read the Visual as Three Interacting Tracks

Software Development Process
Transforms the initial concept into an operational system.
Software Quality Assurance
Checks that activities are performed properly and artifacts meet requirements and quality standards.
Software Project Management
Controls and administers development and SQA work.

Important: these are interacting tracks—not Development → then SQA → then Project Management.

Connect the Tracks to PQCT

Development creates the product, SQA provides confidence in process and artifacts, and project management controls effort, schedule, risk and administration. Together they aim to improve Productivity and Quality while controlling Cost and Time to Market.

Map the Responsibility

Classify: designing an architecture; reviewing a requirements document; estimating effort and scheduling a release.

Self-feedback: Development → architecture. SQA → review for conformance/quality. Project Management → estimation and scheduling.
