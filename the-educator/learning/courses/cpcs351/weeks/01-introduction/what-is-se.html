What Is Software Engineering?

Software engineering applies systematic engineering processes and methods to create, deliver, and evolve software while balancing productivity, quality, cost, and time.

3of 11 pages
Before You StartIf a program compiles, passes a demo, and satisfies one developer, has it been engineered?

Look for the difference between producing code and producing a dependable software product.

Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning Focus

Define software engineering, distinguish it from programming, and explain how PQCT expresses engineering trade-offs.

Core Lecture

From Programming to Engineering

  • The discipline applies engineering processes and methods to software.
  • Its practical objective is PQCT: productivity, quality, cost, and time to market.
  • Engineering adds repeatability, visibility, coordination, and disciplined decision-making to software work.

Worked Example

Online Payment Service

The team does more than implement transactions: it defines requirements, designs interfaces, reviews changes, tests releases, measures defects, manages risk, and coordinates deployment. Those activities make the work engineering rather than isolated coding.

Remember

Software engineering is engineering thinking applied to the full software product and process—not a synonym for coding.

Common Misconception

“Software engineering means more documentation and meetings.” Documentation and coordination are useful only when they reduce uncertainty, manage complexity, or provide evidence. The goal is better engineering outcomes, not paperwork.
