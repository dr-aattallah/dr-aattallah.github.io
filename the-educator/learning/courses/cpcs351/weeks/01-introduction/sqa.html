Topic 01 · Lesson 07

Software Quality Assurance & Project Management

Development builds the system. SQA asks whether the work and artifacts meet expectations; project management asks whether the work is controlled and deliverable.

7of 8 lessons
Two different safety netsA project can produce high-quality code and still miss the deadline. It can also finish on time and deliver poor quality. Who watches each problem?
Learning Focus

Distinguish SQA from project management and identify the responsibilities listed for each track in the chapter.

Visual Anchor · Quality Gate vs Control Tower

Quality Gate · SQA

Ensures development activities are performed properly and that software artifacts meet software requirements and desired quality standards.

Control Tower · Project Management

Oversees the control and administration of development and SQA activities so delivery remains manageable.

Project Management Activities in the Chapter

  • Effort estimation
  • Project planning and scheduling
  • Risk management
  • Project administration
  • Other control and coordination activities needed to deliver on time and within budget

Spot the Responsibility

“The requirements review found an ambiguous rule.” “The release is two weeks behind schedule.” Which track primarily addresses each?

Self-feedback: The review finding is primarily SQA; schedule control is primarily project management. The tracks interact, but their purposes are different.
Remember

SQA protects process/artifact quality; project management controls the project. Neither replaces development.
