Where do software's cost and difficulty come from?

Must Know

Why software is different

IntangibleProgress and effort are harder to inspect than a physical product.
Easy to reproduceCopying is cheap; most cost lies in development.
Labor-intensiveMuch intellectual work is difficult to automate completely.
Easy to modifyChanges are tempting, but their wider consequences may be hidden.
Must Know · continued

Working code is not enough

Anyone can hack something togetherA program may appear to work while serious quality problems remain hidden.
Software does not physically wear outIt deteriorates when changes increase complexity or violate the design.
Official Idea, Explained

Copying is cheap.
Creating is not.

The expensive part is discovering what to build, designing it, implementing it, checking it, coordinating people, and changing it safely.

Apply It · Banking App

Can one “small” rule create a big engineering change?

A bank adds one rule: international transfers above a threshold require an additional approval. What parts of the system could this affect?

UI · business logic · authorization · audit records · tests · operational procedures

Key Takeaway

Software's unusual economics and changeability are exactly why disciplined engineering is needed.
