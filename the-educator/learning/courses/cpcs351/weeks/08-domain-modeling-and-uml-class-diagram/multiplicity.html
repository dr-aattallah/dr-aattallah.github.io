Multiplicity & Relationship Constraints

Translate business rules into precise cardinality constraints at association ends.

9of 14
  • Multiplicity states how many instances may participate at an association end.
  • 1 = exactly one; 0..1 = zero or one; * / 0..* = zero or more; 1..* = one or more.
  • m..n means between m and n; n means exactly n.
  • Lecture example: one Base Station services zero or more Mobiles, while every Mobile is serviced by exactly one Base Station.
  • Self-association example: an Employee may supervise zero or more other Employees, with supervisor and worker roles.
