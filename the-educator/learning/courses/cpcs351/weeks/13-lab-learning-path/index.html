Software Engineering Labs

The lab component turns lecture concepts into engineering work. Across twelve labs, students interpret scenarios, use UML/CASE tools, build analysis and design artifacts, justify engineering decisions, and transfer selected work into the group project.

10%Course grade allocated to Lab Activities in the current syllabus.
12 LabsA progressive path from UML foundations and requirements to architecture, patterns and design principles.
Learn → ApplyEach lab combines a focused concept, guided examples or practice, and evidence of student work.
How the lab works

From concepts to engineering evidence

The laboratory is not a second lecture. Students are expected to use tools and models to solve concrete software-engineering problems. Some source labs are practice-oriented; others explicitly contain graded work. The twelve redesigned guides below preserve the full instructional intent and activities of the original lab files while rewriting and reorganizing them for clearer study.

Assessment note: the course syllabus assigns 10% to Lab Activities. Individual source lab sheets sometimes identify particular exercises as graded and may mention marks for those activities; those local instructions are retained inside the relevant lab guide rather than being treated as a new course-wide grading scheme.
