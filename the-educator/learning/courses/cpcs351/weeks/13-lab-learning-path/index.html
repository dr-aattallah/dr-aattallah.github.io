Software Engineering Labs

The lab component turns lecture concepts into engineering work. Across twelve labs, students interpret scenarios, use UML/CASE tools, build analysis and design artifacts, justify engineering decisions, and transfer selected work into the group project.

10%Course grade allocated to Lab Activities in the current syllabus.
12 LabsA progressive path from UML foundations and requirements to architecture, patterns and design principles.
Learn → ApplyEach lab combines a focused concept, guided examples or practice, and evidence of student work.
How the lab works

From concepts to engineering evidence

The laboratory is not a second lecture. Students are expected to use tools and models to solve concrete software-engineering problems. Four labs are formally assessed: Labs 01, 02, 06 and 07. The remaining labs support guided practice, review and skill development. The twelve redesigned guides below preserve the full instructional intent and activities of the original lab files while rewriting and reorganizing them for clearer study.

Assessment note: Lab Activities contribute 10 marks in total. The assessed labs are 01, 02, 06 and 07, worth 2.5 marks each. All other lab guides are learning and practice activities and are not part of these four graded lab assessments.
