Aggregation vs Composition

Both are whole–part relationships, but composition expresses stronger ownership and shared lifecycle.

10of 14
Learning focus

Distinguish aggregation from composition and place the diamond on the whole/aggregate side correctly.

Aggregation

Part-of / has-a

The lecture defines aggregation as a special form of association expressing that one object is part of another object. Example: an Engine is part of a Car. UML uses a clear/white diamond at the aggregate side.

◇ Car — Engine

Engine is part of Car.

Composition

A stronger whole–part relationship

◆ Book — Page

Composition is stronger than aggregation. The parts live and die with the whole.

Exclusive ownership

The lecture describes composition as an aggregated object exclusively owning the part.

Notation trap

The diamond goes on the whole/aggregate side, not on the part side.

Remember

Do not use aggregation/composition merely because one class “uses” another. The relationship must actually mean whole–part.
