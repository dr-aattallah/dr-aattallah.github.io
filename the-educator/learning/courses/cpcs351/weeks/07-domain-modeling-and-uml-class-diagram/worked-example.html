Worked Example: University Registration Domain

Build a domain model incrementally from a short scenario instead of jumping directly to a finished diagram.

12of 14

A department offers courses. Each course may have several sections. An instructor teaches sections. Students register for sections; each registration records status, registration date, and eventually a grade.

  • Candidate classes: Department, Course, Section, Instructor, Student, Enrollment.
  • Candidate attributes: Department.name; Course.code/title; Section.sectionNo/capacity; Student.studentId/name; Enrollment.status/date/grade.
  • Department 1 — 0..* Course.
  • Course 1 — 0..* Section.
  • Instructor 1 — 0..* Section, while each Section has one Instructor in this simplified rule set.
  • Student 1 — 0..* Enrollment and Section 1 — 0..* Enrollment.
  • Enrollment deserves its own domain class because the relationship itself carries state: status, date, and grade.
