The Five-Step Domain Modeling Process

Move from domain information to a reviewed UML class diagram without mixing discovery and drawing too early.

3of 14
  • 1. Collect application-domain information.
  • 2. Brainstorm domain concepts.
  • 3. Classify the brainstorming results.
  • 4. Visualize the domain model using a UML class diagram.
  • 5. Inspect and review the domain model.
  • Lecture tip: do not brainstorm and draw at the same time; list and classify concepts first.
  • A practical team workflow is to capture brainstorming results, have one or two members convert them into UML, circulate the diagram, then revise it from comments.
