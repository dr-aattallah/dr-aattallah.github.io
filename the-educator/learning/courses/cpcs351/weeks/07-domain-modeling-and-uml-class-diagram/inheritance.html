Inheritance, Generalization & Polymorphism

Model IS-A relationships correctly and test inheritance before drawing the generalization arrow.

7of 14
  • Inheritance expresses generalization/specialization between concepts.
  • Vehicle is a generalization of Car; Car is a specialization of Vehicle.
  • Features defined for the superclass are automatically defined for subclasses.
  • IS-A test: every instance of the subclass is also an instance of the superclass.
  • Conformance test: relationships of the superclass must also be valid relationships of subclasses.
  • Lecture counterexamples: Car is not an Engine; Visiting Professor may fail a Professor relationship if visiting professors cannot enroll in the host retirement plan.
  • Polymorphism means one thing can assume different forms; the lecture uses overloading as an example.

Remember

Inheritance is about substitutable kinds, not merely similarity.
