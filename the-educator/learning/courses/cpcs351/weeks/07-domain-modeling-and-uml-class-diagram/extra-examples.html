How to study the gallery

Use the same reading routine every time

  1. Boundary: state what domain the diagram is trying to understand.
  2. Concepts: identify the important classes and separate them from attributes.
  3. Hierarchy: find every IS-A relationship and test whether it is semantically valid.
  4. Connections: classify associations versus aggregation/composition.
  5. Cardinality: translate every multiplicity into a plain-English domain rule.
  6. Explain: summarize the model without pointing at notation—if you can explain the domain, you understand the diagram.

Your challenge

Add multiplicities and explain every one as a sentence about the domain.

Practice rule

A good domain model is not the diagram with the most boxes. It is the smallest model that captures the important domain concepts, their properties, and their relationships clearly enough for shared understanding.
