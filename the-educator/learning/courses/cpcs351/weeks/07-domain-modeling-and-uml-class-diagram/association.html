Association, Roles & Direction

Association is the general UML relationship for domain concepts that participate in an application-specific relationship.

Learning focus

Identify associations and read association names, role names, and direction without confusing association with inheritance or aggregation.

Association

A general relationship between concepts

The lecture defines association as a general, application-specific relationship. Examples include Instructor teaches Course and User has Account.

Quick classification

Association or something stronger?

🔗Association

“Faculty teaches Course” — a domain-specific relationship.

▲Not inheritance

A Faculty member is not a Course.

◇Not aggregation

Neither concept is modeled as a part of the other.

Remember

Use association when two concepts are meaningfully related but the relationship is not an IS-A or whole–part relationship.
