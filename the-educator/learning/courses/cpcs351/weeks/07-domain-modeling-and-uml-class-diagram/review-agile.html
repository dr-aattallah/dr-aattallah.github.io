Exam checklist

You are ready if you can do all of these

  • Define a model and explain why software teams need models.
  • State the five domain-modeling steps in order.
  • Distinguish class, object, attribute, operation, and encapsulation.
  • Explain why a domain model omits operations.
  • Separate candidate classes from attributes using independent existence.
  • Recognize inheritance and apply the IS-A/conformance tests.
  • Identify association, role, direction, and multiplicity.
  • Distinguish aggregation from composition and place the diamond correctly.
  • Construct a small domain model from a scenario and justify each relationship.
Concept check

Five quick questions

  1. Why should brainstorming be separated from UML drawing?
  2. Why is “number of seats” normally an attribute rather than a class?
  3. What two tests must inheritance pass?
  4. What is the meaning of 0..*?
  5. What extra semantic condition makes composition stronger than aggregation?
