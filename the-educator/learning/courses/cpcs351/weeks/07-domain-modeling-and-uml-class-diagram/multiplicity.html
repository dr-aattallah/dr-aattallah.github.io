Multiplicity & Relationship Constraints

Multiplicity turns a relationship line into a precise statement about how many instances may participate.

9of 14
Learning focus

Read and write UML multiplicity constraints such as 1, 0..1, *, 1..*, m..n, and exact n.

Lecture example

Base Station services Mobiles

Base Station
1*services
Mobile

Read it as: one base station services zero or more mobiles, and every mobile is serviced by exactly one base station.

Multiplicity vocabulary

Decode the symbols

NotationMeaning
1Exactly one; also the default when omitted.
0..1Zero or one.
* or 0..*Zero or more.
1..*One or more.
m..nFrom m to n.
nExactly n.
How to read

Read multiplicity from the opposite end

👤Employee supervises Employees

A supervisor may supervise zero or more workers.

🔍Always verbalize

Turn the diagram into a complete sentence; if the sentence sounds wrong, the multiplicity probably is.

Remember

Multiplicity is a constraint on an association. It does not create a new relationship type; it makes an existing relationship more precise.
