Choosing the Right Class Relationship

Choose UML notation from the meaning of the domain relationship—not from diagram aesthetics.

11of 14
  • Inheritance: IS-A generalization/specialization.
  • Association: general application-specific relationship.
  • Aggregation: weak/shared whole–part or has-a.
  • Composition: exclusive whole–part ownership with lifecycle dependence.
  • The lecture notes that UML has additional relationships, but these are the relationships needed here for domain modeling.
