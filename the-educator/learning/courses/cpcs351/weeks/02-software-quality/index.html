Software Quality

Quality is not a final inspection step. It is built through prevention, verification, validation, reviews, testing, measurement, and disciplined improvement.

1of 8 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Define software quality and explain the role of quality-assurance activities in the software process.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • SQA is a set of activities that helps software products and processes conform to requirements and quality standards.
  • Core SQA activities include verification, validation, technical reviews, and testing.
  • Quality has both prevention costs and failure costs; engineering seeks an effective balance.

Worked example

Online banking

Remember

Software quality is engineered across the life cycle, not inspected into the product at the end.
