Cost of Quality

Quality has a cost, but failure has a cost too. SQA invests in prevention because defects become more expensive when discovered later.

3of 8 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Define software quality and explain the role of quality-assurance activities in the software process.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Cost of quality includes prevention costs and failure costs.
  • Prevention costs include establishing the SQA framework, performing SQA activities, monitoring/improving the framework, and needed tools/equipment.
  • Failure costs include analyzing/removing failures, lost developer/customer productivity, liability and lawsuits, higher insurance costs, and damage to reputation.
  • The lecture illustrates that relative error-correction cost rises sharply as defects escape from requirements and design into testing and field operation.

Worked example

Ambiguous payment requirement

Clarifying a requirement during review may take minutes; discovering the same ambiguity after deployment can trigger code fixes, regression testing, customer support, and financial exposure.

Remember

Spend intelligently on prevention and early detection because escaped defects become progressively more expensive.
