From Requirements to Architecture

Requirements tell us what the product must achieve. Architecture makes the first major decisions about how the software will be organized to achieve it.

1of 10 pages
Learning focus

Define software architecture, explain why it matters, and connect requirements, non-functional properties, trade-offs, structure, interfaces, interactions, rationale, and traceability.

Definition

Architecture is both a structure and a decision

🏗️ Software architecture

The architecture of a system or subsystem describes the style of its high-level structure, including the interfaces and interactions among its major components.

🧭 Architectural design

Software architectural design is the decision-making process used to determine the architecture for the system under development.

Key consequence

Different types of systems require different design methods and architectural styles. Architecture is therefore a contextual engineering decision, not a decorative diagram or a universal template.

Three classic lenses

Three ways to remember what architecture captures

🧭

Perry & Wolf

Elements + Form + Rationale

Memory hook: what + how + why.

🧱

Shaw & Garlan

Elements + interactions + composition patterns + constraints.

Architecture explains what the system is built from and the rules shaping how those parts fit together.

🔭

Kruchten

Architecture deals with the high-level structure of software and involves abstraction, decomposition, composition, style and aesthetics.

Combined mental model

Software architecture describes the high-level organization of a software system: what major elements exist, how they interact and compose, what patterns and constraints shape them, and why those structural decisions were made.

What architecture contains

Structure + interaction + rationale

🧩Elements

Subsystems, components and other major building blocks.

🔗Form

How elements connect, interact and compose into a system.

🧭Rationale

Why the design choices were made and which constraints drove them.

🧱Constraints

Rules and non-functional properties that shape the structure.

Why it matters

Architecture amplifies quality decisions

⚡Performance & efficiency

Structure, data flow, communication and deployment decisions can strongly affect system performance and efficiency.

🛡️Security

Boundaries and interfaces determine where protection and trust decisions live.

🧰Maintainability

Good decomposition localizes change; poor decomposition spreads it.

♻️Lifecycle artifact

Architecture is a primary artifact for conceptualizing, constructing, managing and evolving the system.

✓ Sound architecture

Provides a coherent high-level structure for construction, communication, management and future evolution.

↔

⚠ Architectural flaws

Early structural mistakes can propagate across the system and may become serious enough to contribute to project failure.

Trade-offs

There is rarely one “best” architecture

⚖️ Typical trade-offs

  • Schedule ↔ Budget
  • Cost ↔ Robustness
  • Fault tolerance ↔ Size
  • Security ↔ Speed

📝 Engineering discipline

Record important decisions and their rationale, and maintain traceability back to the requirements that justify them.

Memory hook

Requirements define the promise. Architecture defines the high-level structure that makes the promise achievable—and architectural design is the decision process that chooses that structure.
