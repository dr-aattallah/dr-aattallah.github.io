Cohesion, Coupling, KISS & Abstraction

Good design keeps responsibilities focused, dependencies controlled, solutions simple, and unnecessary detail hidden.

13of 14 pages
Learning focus

Evaluate a design using four complementary principles: high cohesion, low coupling, KISS, and appropriate abstraction.

Design quality

Four questions reveal a lot about a design

🧲

High cohesion

Do the responsibilities inside this module belong together? A cohesive module has a clear, focused purpose.

🔗

Low coupling

How strongly does this module depend on others? Lower coupling limits ripple effects when one part changes.

✨

KISS

Is the solution simpler than it needs to be? Prefer straightforward structures over unnecessary cleverness.

🌫️

Abstraction

Are we showing the essential idea while hiding detail that is not needed at this level?

Cohesion vs coupling

Look inside first, then look between

🧲 Cohesion · inside a module

Good: related responsibilities stay together.

Warning sign: one class or module performs unrelated jobs simply because it is convenient.

↔

🔗 Coupling · between modules

Good: modules communicate through small, stable interfaces.

Warning sign: a small change in one module forces changes across many others.

Design diagnosis

Recognize the bad combination

✕ Low cohesion + high coupling

An “everything” module contains unrelated responsibilities and also depends heavily on many external details. It becomes difficult to understand, test, reuse, and change safely.

→

✓ High cohesion + low coupling

Each module has a focused purpose, exposes a clear interface, hides implementation details, and can change with limited impact on the rest of the system.

KISS + abstraction

Simplify what must be understood now

✨ KISS

Choose the simplest design that satisfies the requirements and quality constraints. Simplicity reduces cognitive load and often improves maintainability and testability.

🌫️ Abstraction

Work at the right level of detail. At architectural level, think in subsystems and responsibilities; at class level, expose only the details needed to understand the class contract.

Quick evaluation

Use this four-step check

1Purpose

Can you state the module's responsibility clearly?

2Dependencies

Does it depend only on what it really needs?

3Simplicity

Can the design be made easier to understand?

4Level

Are unnecessary details hidden at this stage?

Memory hook

Cohesion asks whether things belong together. Coupling asks how strongly modules depend on each other. KISS removes unnecessary complexity. Abstraction hides unnecessary detail.
