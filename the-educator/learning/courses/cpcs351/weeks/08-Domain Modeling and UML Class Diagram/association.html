Association, Roles & Direction

Represent application-specific relationships and make their meaning explicit.

8of 14
  • Association expresses a general relationship between concepts.
  • Associations are often application-specific: Instructor teaches Course; User has Account; Student enrolls in Course.
  • “Enroll” is not inheritance or aggregation; it is an association.
  • An association name states the relationship meaning.
  • Role names identify how each end participates, such as supervisor and worker.
  • Association direction/navigability may be shown when useful.
