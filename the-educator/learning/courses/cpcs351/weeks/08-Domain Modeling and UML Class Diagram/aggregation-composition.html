Aggregation vs Composition

Distinguish weak whole–part relationships from strong exclusive ownership and lifecycle dependence.

10of 14
  • Aggregation expresses that one object is part of another and is a special form of association.
  • It models a part-of / has-a relationship; the lecture uses Engine as part of Car.
  • Shared aggregation is symbolized by a hollow diamond at the aggregate/whole side.
  • Composition is a stronger form of aggregation.
  • Composite parts are exclusively owned and live and die with the whole.
  • Composition is symbolized by a filled diamond; the lecture shows Book–Page and Body–Heart/Liver examples.
Car ◇── Engine
aggregation
Book ◆── Page
composition
Remember

Use composition only when ownership and lifecycle dependence are genuinely strong.
