Why Models and Domain Modeling Matter

Teams need a shared representation because people perceive the same reality differently.

2of 14
  • A model is a conceptual or schematic representation used for further study.
  • Different backgrounds and viewpoints produce different perceptions; the lecture’s blind-men-and-elephant story illustrates this problem.
  • Models facilitate communication between team members and users about reality and design ideas.
  • Models remain useful during enhancement maintenance.
  • Software engineers often enter unfamiliar application domains and therefore need explicit domain knowledge.
  • Domain modeling establishes common understanding and can improve communication with customers and users.
  • The resulting model provides a basis for design, implementation, and maintenance.
