Integration, Deployment, and Maintenance

Subsystems developed separately must be integrated into one working system and deployed into the operational environment.

7of 8 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Differentiate among the phases of software development and understand software engineering within broader system engineering.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Integration exposes mismatched assumptions, interfaces, timing, formats, and physical constraints.
  • System testing evaluates end-to-end behavior across subsystems.
  • Deployment introduces the system into its target operational environment.
  • Maintenance coordinates changes across interdependent hardware, software, and human procedures.

Worked example

Airport upgrade

A new baggage scanner is not complete when its firmware works; conveyor timing, software messages, alarms, staff procedures, and monitoring must also integrate.

Remember

A system succeeds only when its parts work together in the real environment.
