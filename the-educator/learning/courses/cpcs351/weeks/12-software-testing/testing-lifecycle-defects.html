Testing Lifecycle & Defects

Testing is not a single phase after coding. It begins with planning and requirements, continues through execution and defect handling, and finishes with evidence about release readiness.

TEST EARLY · LEARN EARLY

The later a defect survives, the more expensive its impact can become.

Early review of requirements and design can prevent defects before they reach code, integration, system testing, or production.

When do we stop testing?

Not when we have “proved there are no bugs.” Testing is reduced or stopped when agreed exit evidence is sufficient.

CoverageRequired features, requirements, or test cases reach the target.
Risk / DefectsCritical defects are resolved and the remaining risk is acceptable.
ConstraintsRelease deadline, testing window, or budget reaches its agreed limit.
Key connection: Testing finds evidence of problems. Defect tracking makes those problems visible until they are fixed, re-tested, and closed.
