Who Tests? Testing vs Debugging

Testing is a team activity. Separate who participates, how tests are executed, and how much the tester knows about the internals.

Who participates?

🔎Software TesterEvaluates behavior against requirements and reports defects.
⌨DeveloperTests units and investigates failures in code.
▣Project Lead / ManagerUses quality evidence to manage readiness and risk.
◎End UserProvides acceptance evidence from the intended-use perspective.

Testing ≠ Debugging

Testing

Execute software to identify a failure, bug, error, defect, gap, or unexpected behavior.

Question: What failed?
≠
Debugging

Identify, isolate, and fix the cause of the observed problem.

Question: Why did it fail, and how do we fix it?

Two different axes

Execution style: Manual ↔ Automation

Manual testing is performed by a person using scenarios/test cases. Automation uses scripts or tools to rerun checks quickly and repeatedly.

Knowledge: Black ↔ Grey ↔ White Box

This describes how much the tester knows about the application's internal logic and structure.

Black Box
Inputs + outputs
No internal knowledge required
Grey Box
Limited internal knowledge
Partial visibility
White Box
Logic + structure
Internal code knowledge
Quick diagnosis

A developer examines branches inside a method after a test fails. The test exposed the failure; examining and correcting the branches is debugging, using internal/white-box knowledge.

Do not mix the axes: a black-box test can be manual or automated. Manual/automation tells you how; black/grey/white tells you what internal knowledge is used.
