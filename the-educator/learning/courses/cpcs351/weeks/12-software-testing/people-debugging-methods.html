Who Tests? Testing vs Debugging

Separate the roles, execution styles, and visibility of software internals.

2of 8 pages

Who does testing?

The lecture notes that responsibility depends on the process and stakeholders. Large organizations may have dedicated testing teams, while developers perform unit testing. Common participants include the Software Tester, Software Developer, Project Lead/Manager, and End User.

Testing vs debugging

Testing
Identifies bugs/errors/defects without necessarily correcting them.
Debugging
Identifies, isolates, and fixes the problem; normally performed by developers.

Manual vs automation

Manual testing is performed without automated tools/scripts, often using test plans, test cases, scenarios and exploratory testing. Automation testing uses scripts/software to repeat previously performed scenarios quickly and repeatedly.

Black, White & Grey Box

Black Box
No knowledge of internal workings; provide inputs and inspect outputs.
White Box
Detailed investigation of internal logic and code structure; requires internal knowledge.
Grey Box
Testing with limited knowledge of internal workings.
Different axes

Manual/automation describes how tests are executed; black/white/grey describes how much the tester knows about internals.
