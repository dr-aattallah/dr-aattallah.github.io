State Machine Examples

Translate domain stories into stateful behavior before worrying about diagram polish.

4of 12 pages
Lecture example

Book in a library system

NewAvailableReservedBorrowed

Typical events include activate, reserve, borrow, release, and return. Notice that the diagram models the Book object's lifecycle, not the entire library workflow.

Lecture example

Patient in a hospital

A patient enters the hospital and is admitted after checking in. If found healthy, the patient is released; if unhealthy, the patient remains under observation until the diagnosis changes.

Good modeling starts by extracting stable conditions and the events/conditions that move the patient between them.

Richer example

Bank ATM

  • Initially Off; power-on performs startup and enters Self Test.
  • If the test fails → Out of Service; otherwise → Idle.
  • Card insertion moves Idle → Serving Customer.
  • Entering Serving Customer can execute readCard.
  • Cancel can return to Idle; leaving Serving Customer can execute ejectCard.
Added practice

Tic-tac-toe

States such as XTurn and OTurn capture whose turn it is; terminal states such as XWin, OWin, and Tie capture game outcomes. This is a clean example of state-dependent legal behavior.

Modeling discipline

Choose one subject object or subsystem. If your “states” belong to several different objects, the state machine has probably lost its focus.
