Fork, Join, Concurrency & Object Flow

Model parallel work, synchronization, and the movement of information or domain objects through a process.

3of 8 pages
Core lecture

Parallel work and information

  • A synchronization bar can fork one flow into activities that occur in parallel or concurrently.
  • A later synchronization bar joins the concurrent threads.
  • Fork/join is different from decision/merge: parallel obligations versus alternative routes.
  • Activity modeling can show information flow and object flow among activities.
  • An object node may be written as obj:Class; the lecture later shows objects such as :NewOrder and :Invoice.
