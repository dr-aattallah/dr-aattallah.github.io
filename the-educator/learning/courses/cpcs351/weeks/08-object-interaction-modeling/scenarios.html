Before drawing arrows, tell a disciplined object-interaction story.

An interaction sentence has four parts

Subject

Who performs the action?

Action

What does the subject do?

Object acted upon

Who/what receives the action?

Other data / objects

What information is carried?

Construction guidelines

  • Specify the normal scenario first: assume things go as expected.
  • Add alternative flows only when needed.
  • Keep the model simple; leave coding detail to implementation.
  • Leave exception-handling detail to coding.
  • A prototype can be useful when the design idea needs validation.
Why the table matters

It is not bureaucracy. Its columns map naturally to sequence-diagram participants and messages.
