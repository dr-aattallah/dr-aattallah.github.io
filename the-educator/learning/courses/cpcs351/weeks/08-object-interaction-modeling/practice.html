Practice Studio

Practice the reasoning pipeline, not just UML notation.

🎓Challenge A · Registration

Student requests a section. Identify nontrivial steps; write normal scenario/table; choose participants; add prerequisite, capacity and conflict guards; create Enrollment.

🅿️Challenge B · Smart Parking

Driver requests a space, pays, and receives reservation. Separate foreground from background before drawing.

🧩Challenge C · Repair causality

A diagram calls getUser(uid) before the actor submits uid. Explain why the semantics are wrong and repair the order.

Submission recipe

  1. Underline the actor trigger.
  2. Mark trivial vs nontrivial use-case steps.
  3. Write the normal scenario.
  4. Convert at least six actions into a five-column scenario table.
  5. Draw participants and messages in causal order.
  6. Add guards/loop/create only where justified.
  7. Explain two receiver responsibilities implied by messages.
  8. Review the diagram for the mistakes in Lesson 14.
Self-test

If you can explain why every arrow exists and why it occurs at that exact point in time, you understand the model. If you only recognize the symbols, you do not yet.
