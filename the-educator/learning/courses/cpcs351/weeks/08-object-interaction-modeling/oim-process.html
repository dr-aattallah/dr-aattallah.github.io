Lesson 04

The OIM Process & Nontrivial Steps

Model selectively: first find the use-case steps that actually require background processing.

The five lecture steps

1 · Collect

Collect information about business processes.

2 · Describe

Describe scenarios for nontrivial steps.

3 · Structure

Convert a scenario to a scenario table if useful.

4 · Visualize

Convert the scenario/table to a sequence diagram when the visualization adds value; the lecture treats the OIM sequence diagram as optional.

5 · Inspect

Check the OIM model for desired properties.

How do I identify a nontrivial step?

SignalClassificationReason
System simply displays a menu/input dialogTrivialNo background processing.
Same fixed response for all actorsUsually trivialPresentation dominates.
Response differs by actor/data/conditionNontrivialProcessing is needed to determine the response.
Objects must retrieve/check/create/update/saveNontrivialClearly goes beyond GUI behavior.
Mark it in the expanded use case

The lecture recommends identifying and marking nontrivial steps in the right column, then beginning the scenario with the step on the left that triggers that processing.
