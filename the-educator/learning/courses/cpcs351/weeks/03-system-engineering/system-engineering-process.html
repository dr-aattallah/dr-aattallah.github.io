System Engineering Process

The process moves from mission and requirements to architecture, subsystem development, integration, deployment, and maintenance.

2of 8 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Differentiate among the phases of software development and understand software engineering within broader system engineering.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • System requirements definition identifies business goals, current situation, needs, constraints, and system capabilities.
  • Architectural design decomposes the system and allocates requirements to subsystems.
  • Hardware, software, and human-resource subsystems may be developed by different engineering teams.
  • System integration and testing establish that the assembled system works together.
