Architectural Design Diagrams

Different diagrams reveal different architectural questions; choose the notation that makes the needed relationship clear.

6of 8 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Differentiate among the phases of software development and understand software engineering within broader system engineering.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • The lecture presents block diagrams, UML component diagrams, SysML block-definition/internal-block diagrams, and data-flow diagrams with material flows.
  • Block diagrams emphasize major components and connections.
  • UML component diagrams emphasize software/component dependencies and provided/required interfaces.
  • SysML is useful for multidisciplinary systems with physical and logical blocks.
  • Data-flow/material-flow views emphasize movement of information or physical items.

Remember

Use the diagram that answers the engineering question; no single notation shows everything well.
