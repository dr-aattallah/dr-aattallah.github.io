Agile Methods

Agile is a family of methods and practices rather than one single process.

10of 11 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Describe the different software development models: waterfall, prototype, evolutionary, spiral, and agile.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • The lecture lists DSDM, Feature Driven Development (FDD), Scrum, Extreme Programming (XP), Crystal Clear, and Lean Development.
  • Different methods emphasize different practices: planning cadence, features, engineering discipline, team communication, or waste reduction.
  • Scrum provides a concrete iterative framework; XP adds strong technical practices such as frequent integration and testing.
