08 · SOFTWARE DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Design Principles

Use these two visual study summaries to review the principles that guide cleaner object-oriented design before moving into GRASP responsibility assignment. Focus on the design problem each principle prevents, the structural idea behind it, and the effect on maintainability and change.

Recognize the problemIdentify designs with mixed responsibilities, rigid extension points, unsafe inheritance, oversized interfaces, or excessive dependencies.
Apply the principleUse SRP, OCP, LSP, ISP, DIP, Low Coupling, and High Cohesion as reasoning tools—not mechanical rules.
Connect to GRASPThese principles prepare you to judge whether Controller, Information Expert, and Creator responsibilities are assigned well.
Study takeaway: good design principles do not prescribe one perfect class structure. They help you evaluate alternatives: keep responsibilities focused, dependencies controlled, abstractions stable, and change localized.
