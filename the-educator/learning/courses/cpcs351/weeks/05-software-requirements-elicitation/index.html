The central idea

From stakeholder reality to an agreed specification

  • Requirements are capabilities the system must deliver.
  • Requirements elicitation identifies and formulates the real capabilities and constraints for the future software system.
  • Requirements are documented in a specification that may form part of a contract.
  • The planning phase includes elicitation, deriving use cases, and producing an iterative development plan.
  • The team works closely with customers and users to understand needs, determine real requirements, and prioritize them.
Why it matters

Requirements mistakes multiply downstream

📜 Contract

A requirements specification can carry legal and commercial consequences.

💸 Rework

A late discovery can force redesign, recoding and retesting.

📊 Project risk

The lecture uses the Standish CHAOS study to emphasize how strongly requirements problems can affect projects.

Worked example

Clinic scheduling

Problem: patients struggle to find appointments. Wish: “make booking easier.” Requirement: “The system shall let a patient search available appointments by specialty, physician and date and confirm a selected slot.”

Remember

Elicitation does not record wishes verbatim. It discovers the underlying need and converts it into requirements precise enough to design, build and test.
