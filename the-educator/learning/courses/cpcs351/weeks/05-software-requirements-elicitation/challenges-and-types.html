Why Requirements Are Hard — and What We Must Specify

Stakeholders speak in goals, frustrations and wishes; engineers must turn them into precise capabilities, qualities and constraints.

2of 9 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Explain the major elicitation challenges and distinguish functional from non-functional requirements.

Why elicitation fails

Different people hold different pieces of the problem

  • Customers, users and developers have different backgrounds, terminology and assumptions.
  • The team may not know the application domain; customers may not know what software can realistically do.
  • Needs are incomplete, conflicting and change throughout the lifecycle.
  • Software development is often a wicked problem: learning more can change how the problem itself is understood.
  • Non-functional requirements are often understated even though they may determine usability, security, safety or scalability.
Two requirement families

What the system does vs. how well and under what limits

⚙️ Functional requirements

Statements of the information-processing capabilities the software must possess.

Official example: a car-rental system shall let a customer inquire about available cars using make, model, dates, price range and class.

🛡️ Non-functional requirements

Performance, quality, safety, security and interface requirements, plus relevant design or implementation constraints.

⏱ Performance⭐ Quality🦺 Safety🔒 Security🔌 Interface
Official examples

Make qualities measurable where possible

Performance

Handle 10,000 simultaneous inquiries; response time ≤ 3 seconds under typical workload.

Quality & constraints

Protect user privacy and website contents; support specified platforms, databases and user-interface channels.

Worked classification

University admissions

Functional: applicants upload documents and submit an application. Performance: 95% of page requests complete within 2 seconds under 5,000 concurrent users. Security: only authorized admissions staff may view confidential documents.

Memory hook

Functional = capability. Non-functional = quality or constraint. Classify by engineering intent, not by keywords alone.
