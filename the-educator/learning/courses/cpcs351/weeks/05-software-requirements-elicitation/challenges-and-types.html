Challenges and Types of Requirements

The hard part is not collecting wishes; it is discovering precise, shared, feasible requirements despite communication and uncertainty.

2of 9 pages
Core lecture content Clarification & study aids
Learning focus

Justify functional and non-functional requirements and apply elicitation/analysis methods to produce software requirements.

Core lecture

What you need to understand

  • Requirements elicitation is difficult because customers, users, and developers have different backgrounds and terminology.
  • Misunderstanding, incomplete knowledge, changing needs, and “wicked” problem characteristics create uncertainty.
  • Functional requirements specify information-processing capabilities.
  • Nonfunctional requirements include performance, quality, interface, design, and implementation constraints.
Visual model

See the idea

Functional → what the system does
Nonfunctional → how well / under what constraints
Worked example

Car rental

Functional: allow a customer to reserve an available car. Nonfunctional: handle 10,000 simultaneous inquiries and protect customer privacy.

Remember

Separate required behavior from required qualities and constraints, then make both precise enough to assess.
