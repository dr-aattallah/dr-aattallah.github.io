Privacy & security

Core definitions

Privacy concerns an individual or group controlling and selectively revealing information about themselves. Information security protects information and information systems from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification, inspection, recording or destruction.

Three privacy practices

Data Minimization

Limit collection and retention of personal information to what is necessary for a specific purpose. Example: an online shop collects only name, address and payment information needed for checkout.

Anonymization

Remove or encrypt personally identifiable information so individuals cannot be identified. Example: researchers anonymize patient data before analysis.

User Consent

Individuals should control how personal information is collected, used and shared. Example: a mobile app asks the user to grant or deny location access.

Information Security: CIA

Confidentiality

Protect sensitive information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Integrity

Assure that data remains accurate, consistent and unaltered.

Availability

Keep data and resources accessible and usable when needed.
