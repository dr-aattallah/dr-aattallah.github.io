Password Policy

Recommended Password Policy

Recommended Password Policy

Length

8 characters or more, presented in the lecture as a way to slow brute-force attacks.

Complexity

Use characters from 3 of 4 categories: uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and symbols, while avoiding symbols with special system uses.

Avoid predictable passwords

Avoid dictionary words, simple patterns such as 12345678 or 11111111, and easily guessed information such as birthdays or family names.

Story checkpoint

“Jeddah1984” feels memorable. That is the problem.

The lecture warns against meaningful dictionary words, simple patterns and easily guessed personal information. Its recommended policy combines sufficient length with character-category complexity.
