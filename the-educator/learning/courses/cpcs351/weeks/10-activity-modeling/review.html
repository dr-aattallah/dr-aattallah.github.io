When to Use Activity Diagrams & Agile Review

Finish by choosing the right behavioral diagram and keeping activity modeling lightweight, collaborative, and purposeful.

8of 8 pages
Core lecture

When activity diagrams are the right tool

  • Use them for analyzing use cases.
  • Use them to understand workflows that span many use cases.
  • Use them for multi-threaded applications and processes with substantial concurrency.
  • Do not use them primarily to show how objects collaborate; sequence/collaboration diagrams are better for that.
  • Do not use them primarily to show how one object behaves over its lifetime; state diagrams are better for that.
Final comparison

When to use what?

Sequence / Collaboration
interactions and dialog structure
State
lifetime, states, reactive behavior
Activity
business/system processes and concurrency
Agile principles

Model barely enough

  • Value working software over comprehensive documentation.
  • Active user involvement is imperative.
  • Use a collaborative and cooperative approach among stakeholders.
  • Capture requirements at a high level; keep them lightweight and visual.
  • Do barely enough activity modeling to clarify the workflow.
