Lesson 05

Receiving Orders, Supply & Iteration

Model repeated line-item processing, guarded business rules, and the interaction between order fulfillment and incoming stock.

5of 8 pages
Core lecture

Receiving an order

  • Receive order and process each line item.
  • Check each line item: if in stock, assign it to the order; if it needs reorder, trigger Reorder Item.
  • Authorize Payment on its own guarded path; failure may cancel the order.
  • Dispatch only when stock is assigned to all line items and payment is authorized.
  • The lecture marks iteration with * for each line item.
Core lecture

Receiving supply

  • Choose outstanding order items that can be filled from incoming supply.
  • Assign goods to those orders and repeat for each selected item.
  • If all outstanding items for an order are filled, the order may be dispatched.
  • Put remaining goods into stock.
  • The combined diagram connects order processing and supply processing.
