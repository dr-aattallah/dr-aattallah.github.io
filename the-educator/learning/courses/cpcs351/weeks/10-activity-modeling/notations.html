Activities, Decisions, Guards & Flow

Read the fundamental notation used to express work, sequencing, alternatives, iteration, and completion.

Core lecture

Notation essentials

  • The core symbol is an activity/action: a task that needs to be done.
  • Activities are linked by control flow; many transitions occur when the previous computation finishes.
  • A decision is shown by a diamond and outgoing paths are guarded by conditions.
  • A merge recombines alternative routes.
  • Iteration can be represented by a repeated path; the lecture illustrates repetition using * on a trigger.
  • Initial, activity-final, and flow-final nodes define entry and termination semantics.
