How The Educator works

A simple guide for instructors and students to understand the main flow of the platform.

Main idea

InstructorCreate and manage
StudentView and submit
CourseOne organized space

Core features

Course spaces

Each course has one clean workspace for syllabus, files, slides, links, assignments, announcements, and grades.

Resources

Instructors add resources and decide whether they are visible to students.

Assignments

Students open assignments, read instructions, submit files, and later see feedback.

Grades

Instructors review submissions and maintain a gradebook. Students view only their own grades.

Basic flow

1. Instructor creates a course space from the dashboard.
2. Instructor adds resources, assignments, announcements, and students.
3. Student enters the course and opens materials or assignments.
4. Instructor reviews submissions, grades work, and publishes feedback.
