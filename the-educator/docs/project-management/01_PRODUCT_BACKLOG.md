## Epic 1: Course Management

### EDU-CM-001 — Create Course
**As a** professor,  
**I want** to create a new course by entering course name, course code, academic year, semester, and Gregorian year,  
**so that** I can manage all teaching resources and activities for that course.

**Priority:** High  
**Status:** Proposed

### EDU-CM-002 — Manage Course Content
**As a** professor,  
**I want** to add, edit, delete, enable, disable, and organize course content,  
**so that** I can keep my course resources flexible and updated over time.

**Priority:** High  
**Status:** Proposed

### EDU-CM-003 — Upload Syllabus
**As a** professor,  
**I want** to upload and manage a course syllabus,  
**so that** students and course records have a clear official reference for the course structure.

**Priority:** High  
**Status:** Proposed

### EDU-CM-004 — Upload Course Files
**As a** professor,  
**I want** to upload PDFs, PowerPoint slides, videos, and external links,  
**so that** all course resources are stored and organized in one place.

**Priority:** High  
**Status:** Proposed

### EDU-CM-005 — Optional Learning Outcomes
**As a** professor,  
**I want** learning outcomes to be optional and configurable,  
**so that** I can enable, disable, add, or hide them depending on the course needs.

**Priority:** Medium  
**Status:** Proposed

## Epic 2: Research Tracking

### EDU-RT-001 — Connect Academic Profiles
**As a** professor,  
**I want** the system to connect to Google Scholar, ORCID, and ResearchGate,  
**so that** it can track my personal research profile.

**Priority:** High  
**Status:** Proposed

### EDU-RT-002 — Automatic Research Updates
**As a** professor,  
**I want** the system to automatically fetch new publications and research updates,  
**so that** my research record stays current without manual entry.

**Priority:** High  
**Status:** Proposed

### EDU-RT-003 — Research Metrics Summary
**As a** professor,  
**I want** the system to show key research metrics such as publications, citations, and profile indicators,  
**so that** I can quickly understand my academic research status.

**Priority:** Medium  
**Status:** Proposed
